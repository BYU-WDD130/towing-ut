Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At 24/7 Towing Services Utah ("we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website 247towingservicesut.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information when you voluntarily provide it to us by filling out our dispatch or quote forms, which includes your full name, phone number, vehicle details, and location.

2. Use of Information

The information collected is used exclusively to contact you for roadside assistance dispatch, coordinate towing services, and provide emergency support.

3. Text Messaging (SMS)

By providing your phone number on our website, you consent to receive SMS communications or calls regarding your dispatch request. We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information or SMS consent data with third parties or marketing agencies for promotional purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement technical security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about our privacy policy, please contact us through our main dispatch line.
